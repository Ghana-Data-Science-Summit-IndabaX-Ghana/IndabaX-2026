## Lenders must disclose total cost of credit before signing
The Borrowers and Lenders Act 2020 (Act 1052) requires every licensed lender in Ghana
to give you a written disclosure of the total cost of credit before you sign a loan
agreement. This disclosure must include the loan amount, the interest rate expressed
as an Annual Percentage Rate (APR), all fees and charges, the total amount repayable,
and the repayment schedule. You have the right to receive this information and to
take time to read it before committing. Do not sign an agreement that lacks this
disclosure. If a lender refuses to provide it, report the institution to the Bank of Ghana.

## APR versus monthly rate quoting
APR (Annual Percentage Rate) is the legally required standard for disclosing interest
in Ghana. It accounts for compounding and fees across the full year, making it the
only reliable way to compare loans from different lenders. A lender that quotes only
a monthly rate — for example "just 4% per month" — may be obscuring a much higher
effective annual cost. Four percent per month compounded is approximately 60% APR.
Always convert any quoted monthly or weekly rate to APR before comparing. The lender
is required to provide the APR; if they decline to state it, treat that as a warning sign.

## Proportionate collateral
Under the Borrowers and Lenders Act 2020, a lender may not demand collateral whose
value is disproportionate to the loan amount. For small farm-input loans, collateral
requirements must be reasonable and proportionate. If a lender demands collateral
worth many multiples of the loan, you may challenge this under the Act. The Bank of
Ghana Consumer Protection Guidelines reinforce that collateral demands must be fair
and not exploitative.

## How to spot hidden costs
Common hidden costs to watch for include: processing or administration fees not
included in the stated APR; mandatory insurance policies that benefit the lender
but are charged to you; account opening or maintenance fees tied to loan disbursement;
and prepayment penalties buried in fine print. Request a full written schedule of
every fee, charge, and penalty before signing. If the total repayable amount on the
written schedule is higher than what you calculated from the APR and principal, ask
the lender to explain every line item.

## Your right to a copy of the contract
You are entitled to a copy of the signed loan agreement. Keep it in a safe place.
If a dispute arises, the written contract is the reference. If a lender refuses to
give you a copy, this is a violation of your rights under the Act.

## Where to report violations
If a licensed lender violates your rights under the Borrowers and Lenders Act 2020,
you can report to the Bank of Ghana's Consumer Affairs department. Your district
MoFA extension officer can also direct you to legal aid or consumer protection
resources. This assistant provides information only; for legal disputes, consult a
qualified legal professional or the Bank of Ghana directly.
